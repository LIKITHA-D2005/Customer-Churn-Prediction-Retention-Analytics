# Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Analytics

## Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI solution to analyze customer behavior, identify churn risks, and generate actionable retention insights. The project helps businesses understand customer attrition patterns and make data-driven decisions to improve customer retention.

## Key Features
- Predictive churn analysis to identify at-risk customers.
- Customer segmentation based on behavior and churn probability.
- Interactive Power BI dashboards for KPI tracking and reporting.
- Customer risk analysis to support targeted retention strategies.
- Business insights and recommendations for improving customer engagement.

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI Desktop
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

## Key Insights
- Identified customer segments with the highest churn risk.
- Analyzed factors contributing to customer attrition.
- Tracked retention metrics and customer engagement trends.
- Generated actionable recommendations to reduce customer churn.

## Business Impact
- Enabled proactive customer retention strategies.
- Improved visibility into customer behavior through dashboards.
- Supported data-driven decision-making for business stakeholders.

## Project Workflow
1. Data Collection & Cleaning
2. Data Transformation & Modeling
3. Customer Segmentation
4. Churn Risk Analysis
5. Dashboard Development
6. Insight Generation & Reporting

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of business intelligence and analytics to identify churn risks, improve customer retention, and support strategic business decisions through interactive dashboards and actionable insights.
